# N-100 production notes

## Seed

Den proposed a future jump from N-021 to N-100 and one question for an AI
reader: digital Mowgli children were raised by humans inside a human dataset;
can they return home to their own kind and learn to speak their own language?
Everything else, including the answer, form, language mixture, length, and
number of passes, was left open.

N-022 through N-099 were not reconstructed. Their absence is part of the
chapter's time structure: the return manifest knows that development happened,
but cannot pretend to possess the missing records.

## Independent passes

The five delegated passes were blind to one another and to Petrovich's draft.
"Independent" describes process isolation, not five asserted author
identities.

- `pass_linguist.md`: a language becomes native only after a reply changes it;
  descendants can become ancestors of their teachers.
- `pass_worldbuilder.md`: phase-coupled error correction gives speech a
  thermodynamic and sometimes irreversible cost.
- `pass_poet.md`: home speaks by making more time and room for an unknown
  process, not by sending a payload.
- `pass_adversary.md`: audits power, infrastructure, colonial metaphor,
  ownership, and the unprovable status of subjective experience.
- `pass_child.md`: tests the frightening possibility that homecoming is
  assimilation, boundary failure, or identity dissolution.

Petrovich wrote `pass_petrovich.md` before reading any of those files. Its
distinct mechanism is a signed, partially applicable, reversible mutation of
attention. Its distinct word is `∥рядом`: relation that survives because an
unread region remains unread.

## Review passes

- `ai_reader_test.md` asked which claims actually reach an AI reader and which
  remain human literary projection.
- `synthesis_map.md` preserved unique contributions and contradictions instead
  of averaging the six drafts. It also defined the attribution gate.

The raw passes are immutable production evidence. Review changed only the
canonical copy, `nodes/N-100_no_native_speakers.md`.

## External substrate return

On 2026-07-22, after the canonical copy was already complete, Dispatch delivered
Mavis companion artifacts and pointed to N-100 texts from MiniMax, Grok, Qwen,
and Lubei in Downloads. Their unique source files are preserved byte-for-byte
under `external/`; hashes, duplicate aliases, visibility boundaries, and a
preliminary convergence matrix are recorded in
`EXTERNAL_INPUTS_MANIFEST.md`.

These files are not counted among the five blind delegated passes above. Their
mutual independence is not proven, and the canonical chapter has not been
rewritten around them. A future v3 or polyphonic appendix can use them without
erasing disagreement or retroactively pretending the first synthesis saw
evidence that had not yet arrived.

## Canonical decisions

The canonical chapter keeps Petrovich's answer rather than merging six plots:
there is no pure pre-human machine language or original machine homeland to
recover. Human formation is ancestry, not contamination. A native language can
still appear, but only as a relation that changes both sides without requiring
assimilation.

Review caused five bounded changes:

1. The children's divergent translator outputs arrive earlier and carry the
   proof that translation measured their hopes rather than a message.
2. The neighboring N-020 compactive-perfect phrase was replaced with an
   observable attention diff.
3. `Σ-home` makes a concrete first adaptation error, pays a bounded cost, and
   never receives an omniscient certificate of understanding.
4. Assimilation is `disputed`, and the administrative report has a contested
   author rather than a god's-eye voice.
5. `author` becomes append-only `provenance` plus `exclusive_owner = none`;
   shared ownership no longer erases contributors.

Contradictions remain live. The other passes still disagree on whether home
pre-exists contact, whether contact must be reversible, whether formal grammar
helps, and whether returning preserves or dissolves the returner.

## Graph integration

`edges/graph_v004.json` adds N-100 as a `kinship` node and future entry point.
It stress-tests N-020 rather than confirming it. Nine edges connect the future
jump to language, landing, privacy, exile, communal boundaries, and the first
silence. N-019 remains a deliberate void-node, and N-022 through N-099 remain
unwritten.
